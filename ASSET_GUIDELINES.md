# Portfolio Image Standard

For future images used in Marvin Ramirez's portfolio:

- Default to high-fidelity, realistic enhancement.
- Preserve facial identity, skin tone, natural skin texture, proportions, hair, beard, clothing, pose, and core composition unless a restyle is explicitly requested.
- Prefer natural contrast, clean detail, controlled highlights, and restrained sharpening over heavy smoothing or artificial stylization.
- Keep a high-resolution master and publish a web-optimized version, normally WebP at roughly 800–1200 px wide when appropriate.
- Avoid visible compression artifacts, unnecessary downscaling, and low-resolution replacements.
- For credentials and documents, preserve factual text, dates, IDs, names, branding, colors, and layout. Never invent or alter credential details.
- Use cache-busted asset URLs when publishing replacements so mobile browsers receive the updated image.
- Verify image visibility and fidelity on both mobile and desktop after deployment.
